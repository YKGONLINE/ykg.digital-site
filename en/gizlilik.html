Privacy and Data Protection

This notice explains how we use personal data when you visit ykg.digital, use the contact form or apply for the ATLAS beta. For your measurement and advertising preferences, see the Cookies and Measurement page.

What information do we receive?

When you visit the site, the following information is processed electronically and automatically: your IP address, browser and device information (browser type, language and screen size), the address and title of the page you open, the address of the page that brought you to this site, and your measurement and advertising preferences.

When you use a form, we receive your name, the email address or telephone number you provide, and your message directly from you. Please do not include identity numbers, payment details or sensitive information unnecessary for your request.

Contact form

We use the information you share to read and answer your request, assess what you need and continue the conversation you started. We do not add your contact details to a separate marketing list.

Messages are processed through Google services and Google Workspace so we can receive and answer them.

ATLAS beta application

Applications are intended only for people working in real estate. We use your name, contact details and message to assess your application, understand your needs relating to ATLAS and contact you about the beta process. Applying does not guarantee acceptance or immediate access.

We do not add your application details to marketing lists. Messages are processed through the same Google services as the contact form.

Form security

We use a daily submission limit through Cloudflare to protect the contact and ATLAS beta forms from abuse. The first two submissions from an internet connection do not show a bot verification screen; Turnstile verification is required from the third submission onward. Your IP address is processed for the daily limit, and browser/device signals are processed when verification is required. Your name, contact details and message pass through Cloudflare to the Google mail service.

The two forms share a daily limit of 5 submission attempts per internet connection. The day resets on Türkiye time. Our daily counter stores a keyed, day-specific digest rather than the IP address itself; records are deleted within approximately 25 hours. Your name, contact details and message are not stored in counter records. People sharing an office or Wi-Fi connection may share the daily limit.

This security check is separate from measurement and advertising preferences. See Cloudflare’s Turnstile privacy notice for information about its processing.

Legal bases

We process your data on the following legal bases under Article 5 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK):

  • Contact form and ATLAS beta application: the conversation initiated by your request being directly related to establishing a contract (Article 5/2-c), and our legitimate interest in responding to your request (Article 5/2-f).
  • Advertising tools: your explicit consent (Article 5/1). You can withdraw your consent at any time.
  • The hosting provider’s access logs: our legitimate interest in delivering the site securely (Article 5/2-f).
  • Legal retention duties and responding to your KVKK requests: our legal obligation (Article 5/2-ç).

The legal basis for measurement and advertising preferences enabled by default has not yet been established in this draft. A default setting does not establish explicit consent.

Retention

The retention period begins when a message is received. We retain irrelevant messages for one week, answered messages for one month, and messages relating to ongoing conversations or work for up to one year; we delete them when the period expires. If a legal retention duty or dispute applies, the relevant record is retained for as long as that requirement continues.

Analytics cookies and the preference record in your browser are valid for 180 days.

Site measurement and advertising

We measure page visits with Google Analytics 4. Measurement and Analytics cookies are enabled from the first visit, before you make a choice; page-view, IP address, browser and device information reach Google. If you turn measurement off, new page-view events stop, Analytics cookies are removed and the tag will not load on subsequent pages. An already loaded tag may still send technical consent signals.

Our site code sends only page-view events; it does not send a separate measurement event for watching the film or submitting an application. Google Analytics may also process technical session and usage signals.

Google Ads and Meta may be used to measure advertising performance and show ads relevant to your interests. The advertising preference is on by default and can be turned off separately from measurement. This preference will apply once the Google Ads and Meta connections are completed; it does not start advertising tracking on its own. You can change your measurement and advertising preferences on the Cookies and Measurement page.

Service providers and international transfers

We share your data only with the following service providers and for the purposes listed. Their servers are outside Türkiye (primarily in the United States and European Union countries), so your data is transferred abroad. We do not share your data with anyone else, except in response to requests from legally authorised bodies.

  • Google (Google Workspace and Google Apps Script): receiving, storing and answering form messages.
  • Cloudflare (Turnstile and Workers): bot verification, the daily submission limit and securely forwarding form messages to Google.
  • Google (Google Analytics): measuring page views.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages): hosting and delivering the site; your IP address and access logs are processed in this process.

Your rights under the KVKK

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you have the following rights:

  • To learn whether your personal data is being processed.
  • To request information if it has been processed.
  • To learn the purpose of processing and whether your data is used for that purpose.
  • To know the third parties to whom your data is transferred within Türkiye or abroad.
  • To request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data.
  • To request deletion or destruction of your data under the conditions set out in the law.
  • To request that corrections, deletions and destruction be notified to third parties receiving your data.
  • To object to a result against you arising from analysis of your data solely through automated systems.
  • To seek compensation if you suffer damage because your data was processed unlawfully.

How to submit a request

You can submit a written request to the office address above or email ykg@ykg.digital. The telephone and contact form can be used for initial contact; we may need to verify your identity for a formal request.

Requests are free of charge and are answered as soon as possible, within 30 days at the latest. If providing a response incurs an additional cost, a fee may be charged under the tariff determined by the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board. If your request is rejected, you find the response inadequate, or you receive no response within the required period, you may lodge a complaint with the Board.
