SAPIENS 4.0

A way for people to work in the age of AI.

SAPIENS 4.0 is an approach we are developing to explore how people can work with AI. People set the goals, tools support the work, and the final decision stays with people.

PurposeTool supportHuman approvalAction

Purpose comes from people

We begin by deciding what we want to change and who should benefit. We choose tools to serve that purpose.

Work is shared

AI can help with research, organizing information and preparing drafts. People still need time to assess the work and make decisions.

Decision and responsibility stay with people

We assess the result and correct it when needed. A person must approve the work before it is put into use. People decide what gets done and whether the result is acceptable.

We are continuing to develop this approach.
