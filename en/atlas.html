ATLAS

Real estate work moves between conversations, property records and the next thing that needs doing. ATLAS brings them into one workspace.

An agent can describe a client’s request in their own words. ATLAS prepares a record for the agent to review, edit and approve. The decision and control stay with the person doing the work.

Client and property records, notes, reminders and follow-ups are parts of the same work. Photo editing tools and a comparable-property price analysis tool have also been developed for ATLAS. We are continuing to bring them into the agent's day-to-day workflow.

Core records should work even without AI. We want to shape ATLAS with people who work in real estate.
