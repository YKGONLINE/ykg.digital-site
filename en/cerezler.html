Cookies and Measurement

This page explains site measurement, advertising preferences and how to change them. For form data, see Privacy and Data Protection.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Before you choose

No preference panel appears during the opening film. After you skip the film or the skip option has completely disappeared from the screen, a preference icon appears; select it to open the panel. Google Analytics measurement and analytics cookies are enabled from your first visit, before you make a choice. Page views, technical session and usage information are sent to Google, together with your IP address and browser/device information. You can turn measurement off in the preference panel.

Measurement and advertising preferences

Google Analytics uses cookies to measure page views by default. If you turn measurement off, new page-view events stop, Analytics cookies are removed and the tag will not load on subsequent page loads. An already loaded tag may still send technical consent signals. Our site code sends page-view events, rather than separate events for watching the film or submitting an application. Google Analytics may also process technical session and usage signals. Google Ads and Meta may be used to measure advertising performance and show ads relevant to your interests. The advertising preference is on by default and can be turned off separately from measurement. This preference will apply once the Google Ads and Meta connections are completed; it does not start advertising tracking on its own.

Closing the panel leaves your existing settings unchanged. Measurement and advertising preferences are on by default; previous refusals are preserved. “Reject all” turns both preferences off. In “Preferences”, you can change them separately.
