About us

At ykg.digital, we develop our own products and work with people and organizations to bring their ideas to life. We think about brand, communication and technology together. We care as much about how something works as how it looks.

We start by understanding the work. We learn what an organization does, whom it wants to reach, and where it is struggling. For a new idea, we ask who it is for and what need it meets. Those answers guide what we make and how we make it.

Our work can mean shaping a brand identity, building a website, improving existing software or developing a new application. Sometimes the need is to simplify recurring work; sometimes it is to help an organization communicate more clearly. We also design and develop products that are still at the idea stage.

In our own products and our work with others, we want to make things people can use every day—things that make their work easier and evolve as their needs change.

Founder Yusuf Kaan Gültaş ↗
